Why Staying Connected Matters

RJMedia has been built through years of archive preservation, technical experimentation and community engagement.

Like many people across the UK, I experienced housing instability while continuing to maintain archive projects and online resources.

AI helped me remain organised, document information and continue building.

The audience, archive and community only exist while I remain connected.

Every archive update, every community discussion, every project release and every response to someone asking for help depends on reliable technology.

Reliable connectivity is not a luxury. It is the infrastructure that allows the work to continue.

Get in touch

Need Help?

If you are experiencing housing difficulties, digital exclusion, connectivity problems or simply need help organising information, you are welcome to get in touch.

I cannot provide legal advice and cannot guarantee outcomes. I will do my best to share practical lessons, useful resources and information that may help.

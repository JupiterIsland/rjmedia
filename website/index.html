ABOUT RJMEDIA • SOME THINGS ARE WORTH KEEPING

WHY THIS PLACE EXISTS

There used to be thousands of sites like this. Most of them disappeared.

Over the years I've collected, documented, and shared thousands of pieces of music history. Some famous. Some obscure. Some probably only remembered by a handful of people.

This archive exists because once something is gone, it is usually gone forever. Not because they're valuable. Not because they're rare. Mostly because they mattered to somebody. Not everything deserves saving. But some things do.

Every tape on a shelf has a story. — RJM

INDEPENDENT ARCHIVE • NO SECRET BUNKER

SUPPORT RJMEDIA & TAKE IT EASY

Everything here is independent. No massive organisation. No secret archive bunker. No team of experts wearing white gloves. Just a lot of time, curiosity, and the occasional refusal to let something disappear.

If you'd like to support the archive, sponsor a transfer project, donate equipment, or simply spread the word, it genuinely helps. And if you've got an old tape in a cupboard somewhere, don't throw it out just yet.

Communities drift apart.
Websites disappear.
People get older.
Life gets in the way.
It happens.

One memory leads to another.
One recording leads to another.
One person remembers something and suddenly a missing piece of the picture turns up.

Nobody really knows how it happens.

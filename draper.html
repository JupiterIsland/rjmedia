Mansun: The Outsider Thing

It was an "outsider thing". The kind of "outsider thing" that has lured lost souls into the dark labyrinths of obsession throughout the decades... Even alongside rock's lengthy parade of eccentrics and non-conformists, Mansun stood out a mile.

They hailed from Chester, scorned the length of L1 as being the breeding nests of Plastic Scousers - yet even here they were rock kid cast-outs. In 1996, as Blur went Benny Hill and Oasis glammed for supremacy in the Britpop Wars, these enigmatic mis-shapes slunk out of the looking glass bearing numbered EPs of seditious and shimmering 80s flecked pop music.

They made a video for "Taxlo$$" by simply flinging the entire video budget into Liverpool Street station during rush hour and filming the commuters scrambling for the notes. This was a band living by their own rules and conventions, with sonic ambitions far beyond sharing an "acoustic segment" with Paul Weller.

"It was weird," says Paul Draper today, "because it was never, ever a mainstream band, but we were always dipping into that world. It wasn't for me, really. I don't even know how it got that big. Going from making your own piece of 7 inch vinyl to that seemed amazing."

Without fanfare or fuss, Mansun passed away in its sleep in April 2003. Paul disappeared to America, living the Kerouac dream for a few months. Dominic Chad turned to charity. Andie Rathbone started drumming in a new band in Chester. Stove King "left the music business". And there, for any ordinary band, our story would end...

The Grey House Era (2015-2016)

27th April 2016
Spooky Action EP ONE Released
EP ONE is Paul's first record for Kscope and is due to be released on June 10th 2016. The tracks on the EP are: 'Feeling My Heart Run Slow', 'No Ideas' (feat. Steven Wilson), 'The Silence is Deafening', and 'F.M.H.R.S' (The Twilight Sad mix).
28th January 2016
Debut Solo Single Mastered
"Today is a huge milestone in my life. My first ever single is finally finished and is being mastered today along with its b-side and is being delivered to my record company. Thanks to all who helped me do it, you know who you are. I'll be forever in your debt and you've restored my faith in human nature."
17th January 2016
21 Great Prog Rock Albums Coming Your Way In 2016
"The former Mansun man has finally finished work on his first solo album Spooky Action. He expects to announce full details soon. Draper says the album is rather like he's produced himself from the past. He talks about everything that happened to him during his time with Mansun on a set of 15 songs."
10th November 2015
The Anchoress - You & Only You
Steve Lamacq's BBC 6 Music show has an exclusive first play of 'You & Only You feat. Paul Draper', the brand new single from The Anchoress. The track features Paul as guest vocalist and is his first lead vocal performance on a single release in over a decade.
13th September 2015
Paul on 'Spooky Action' Themes
"The whole album is about what happened to me in Mansun, dead simple! It's all about the events that happened in the band and the splitting up and the devastation of the person that was left afterwards. I started writing those words at the back end of 2003 and in 2004. It was a dark place for me, I was drinking a lot, not in a good place."
12th August 2015
Studio Wrap Up
Wrapping up work on Paul's solo album 'Spooky Action'. Musicians in the photo are Ben Stack, Jon Barnett, Ben Sink, and Catherine Anne Davies of The Anchoress (who is missing from the photo but part of the project). Paul has described work as "the most incredible album project of my career so far".

Production: Komakino

Archive Note by Ryan (Singer, Komakino) regarding Paul Draper's 2006 production sessions:

"At the moment we have recorded two songs with Paul as producer - 'Every Day Is Like a Friday Night' and 'Kommunikate'. We met up with Paul in Chester after Steve Lamacq told him that we were looking for a producer... it was fucking weird when we got the email from Paul as we'd been joking about asking him to record us, because being Mansun fans we knew that he'd produced 'Grey Lantern' and 'Six'.

Paul came up to Derby for a couple of days and we bashed through the songs loads of times to try out different structures/rhythmic changes/tempos... he's very much a perfectionist when it comes to that stuff and is really fucking knowledgeable about it all. After that we went up to Liverpool Parr Street Studio for 4 days and did all the instrumental recording."

Archived Fan Q&A (2007)

Question by Eclectic Man
Are you looking to form another band or just go out as a solo artist? And what is the hardest part of the songwriting process?
I'm going to blog the album as I'm making it so everyone can get an inside picture. At the moment I'm starting this project with a blank canvas, using my own name... In terms of the songwriting process itself, I find every aspect of it incredibly difficult, only because I've written hundreds of songs and I have a style, so my main objective is to not repeat myself. Sometimes I can wait for days and nothing will come.
Question by NativeMoon
Hindsight is always 20/20 - But given the chance, if there was one thing about the path you have taken that you would change - what would that be and why?
I think you have no control over your destiny, otherwise you could make decisions on the flip of a coin. I've been very loyal to people I shouldn't have over the years, but what goes around comes around and you'll always end up on the path you were meant to be on. My path is writing songs, so no matter what shit gets thrown, that can be regurgitated into a song as far as I'm concerned.
Question by El Diablo
Will the new material be self-produced by you at your home studio? Have you any plans for any more collaborations?
I'll be producing all my music from now on in a combination of places!! I'm a control freak unfortunately, and I know that alienated the people I've worked with, some for good reasons, some because they couldn't cut it, but there was nobody else to take the lead in getting things sounding great.
Question by MrMavis
What were your favourite and worst venues that you played?
I loved Brixton Academy, Glasgow Barrowlands and venues in other countries that I cant even remember the name of! (Worst venue: Glastonbury).
Question by Candy
What do you think about isolation? What is the longest time you have spent on your own without meeting anyone?
I've spent weeks or possibly months on my own many times, but I love my own company and I can focus on writing when I'm on my own. I'm quite a solitary person, sometimes hermit like, but then I'll go thru a phase of going out every night for ages until my body can't take the hammering any more, and I'll go away and write about my experiences.
